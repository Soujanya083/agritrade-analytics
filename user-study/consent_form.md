# Consent (read aloud, then ask for a yes)

I am a final-year student testing a web application for farmers and buyers. I would like you to try it and tell me
what is easy or hard. It is the **app** that is being tested, not you.

- It takes about 20 to 30 minutes.
- I will write down what you do and say. I will **not** record your name, phone number or photo. You will be "P1", "P2" and so on.
- Your answers are used only in my project report, in summary form.
- You can stop at any time or skip any question without giving a reason, and nothing bad happens.
- Anything you type into the test app is fake practice data.

Do you agree to take part?   Yes / No      (record the yes in your notes, with the date)
