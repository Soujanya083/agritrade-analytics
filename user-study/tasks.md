# Tasks (hand these over one at a time, on paper)

Success = the participant completes it without your help. If you help, note it and count it as a failure.

## Farmers
| # | Task (as read to the participant) | Success when |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | "Create an account and log in." | They reach the dashboard. |
| F2 | "You harvested 200 kg of tomatoes. Put them up for sale at 25 rupees per kg." | The listing appears in their dashboard. |
| F3 | "Find out where it is best to sell your onions." | They open the market / analytics view and say which market it recommends. |
| F4 | "Is now a good time to sell, or should you wait?" | They find the sell-or-wait advice and read it back in their own words. |
| F5 | "Ask the assistant what the price of wheat will be next week." | They get an answer from the assistant. |

## Buyers
| # | Task | Success when |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | "Create an account and log in." | They reach the dashboard. |
| B2 | "Find tomatoes for sale." | They use search or the category filter. |
| B3 | "Place a bid higher than the current price." | The bid shows on the listing. |
| B4 | "You want to see more crops. Show the next ones." | They find "Load more". |

After each task ask: "How easy was that?" 1 (very hard) to 5 (very easy). Add it to the note column.
